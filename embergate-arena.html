About

Embergate Arena is an original real-time lane strategy game. All code, art, names and units are original.

Architecture. The battle runs on a deterministic fixed-timestep simulation (30 Hz) driven entirely by a timestamped command log and a seeded RNG. Both players — you and the Nightbloom AI — issue commands through the same NetAdapter interface the sim validates (energy, hand, deploy zone), which is the anti-cheat/authoritative layer. Because the sim is deterministic, replays are just the seed plus the command log re-simulated. Online play slots in by swapping the local adapter for a WebSocket relay: commands are predicted locally and reconciled when the authoritative tick stream arrives.

Data-driven. Every card is defined in a JSON balance table (cost, HP, damage, speed, range, targeting, abilities) — edit one table to rebalance the whole game.
